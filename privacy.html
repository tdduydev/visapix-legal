VisaPix Privacy Policy

Effective October 30, 2026 · Developer: Duy Tran · Contact: tdduy.dev@gmail.com

Summary

VisaPix turns a photo of you into a passport, ID or visa photo. All face detection, background removal and cropping run on your device. Your photos are never uploaded to us or to anyone else.

Photos and camera

  • You choose a photo from your library or take one with the camera. The app only reads the photo you pick.
  • The photo is processed on your device (Apple Vision on iOS, Google ML Kit with on-device models on Android).
  • Temporary copies are stored in the app's private cache and are removed by the operating system over time. A finished photo is saved to your photo library only when you tap Save, and shared only when you tap Share.
  • We do not have servers that receive photos, and we do not use your photos for training or any other purpose.

Purchases

VisaPix Pro is sold as an in-app subscription through the App Store or Google Play. Payment is handled by Apple or Google; we never see your card details. To check whether you have Pro, the app uses RevenueCat, which receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history from the store. See the RevenueCat privacy policy.

Diagnostics on Android

Google ML Kit, used on Android for on-device face detection and segmentation, sends Google limited diagnostic data such as device model, OS version, API configuration and performance metrics. It does not send your photos. See Google's ML Kit data disclosure.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifiers, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data.

Children

VisaPix is not directed at children under 13. A parent or guardian may use the app to make a photo of their child; the photo stays on the device as described above.

Your choices

You can revoke camera or photo access in your device settings at any time, and delete the app to remove all of its cached data. For questions or requests, email tdduy.dev@gmail.com.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
